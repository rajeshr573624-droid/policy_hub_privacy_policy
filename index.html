PolicyOps

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Last updated: 21 November 2026

PolicyOps (“PolicyOps”, “we”, “us”) is a cloud software service that insurance agencies and brokers (“Agencies”) use to manage their customers, insurance policies, renewals, claims, endorsements, commissions and documents, and to send renewal reminders to their customers, including through the WhatsApp Business Platform. This policy explains what personal data PolicyOps processes, why, who it is shared with, how long it is kept and the choices people have. It applies to the PolicyOps web application at insurance-eb649.web.app and any domain an Agency uses to access it.

1. Who is responsible for the data

  • Agency data. Each Agency decides what information about its customers, policies and staff it enters into PolicyOps and how it is used. For that data the Agency is the data controller (a “Data Fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023) and PolicyOps processes it on the Agency’s behalf and on its instructions (as a “Data Processor”). If you are a policyholder, please contact your insurance agency first about your data.
  • Account data. PolicyOps is responsible for the data needed to run user accounts and the service itself (sign-in details, organization records, security and usage logs).

3. How we use data

  • To provide the service: storing and showing records, search, dashboards, reports, ledgers and commission calculations.
  • To send renewal reminders and other policy notices to customers when an Agency chooses to — manually (Notify) or automatically on the reminder schedule the Agency sets.
  • To create and manage the Agency’s WhatsApp message templates on its WhatsApp Business Account.
  • To manage accounts and access: sign-in, email verification, invitations, roles and permissions, and approving new organizations.
  • To keep the service secure and working: preventing misuse, troubleshooting, backups and audit logs.
  • To meet legal obligations and respond to lawful requests.

We process Agency data only to provide the service to that Agency, under our agreement with it and its instructions.

4. WhatsApp Business Platform and Meta

When an Agency connects its WhatsApp Business number, PolicyOps uses Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API) to send the Agency’s approved message templates to its customers. This includes numbers connected through WhatsApp Business app “coexistence”, where the Agency keeps using the WhatsApp Business app on its phone.

  • What we send to Meta: the customer’s mobile number and the template values needed for the message (for example customer name, policy number, policy type, expiry date, vehicle number and the Agency’s name and contact number), and, if used, a header image.
  • What we receive from Meta: message IDs and delivery or read status, template approval status, and connection details for the Agency’s WhatsApp Business Account.
  • Permissions we use: whatsapp_business_messaging to send messages and whatsapp_business_management to create, check and delete the Agency’s message templates and manage its connection. We use this data only to provide these features to that Agency.
  • Access tokens for the Agency’s WhatsApp Business Account are encrypted and stored on our servers; they are used only by our server-side functions and are not shown in the browser.
  • Messages are sent only on behalf of the Agency, which is responsible for having its customers’ consent (opt-in) to receive WhatsApp messages, as required by WhatsApp’s Business and Commerce policies.
  • Meta processes this data under its own terms and privacy policy: WhatsApp Business Policy and Meta Privacy Policy.

Customers who do not want WhatsApp messages can ask their Agency to stop them, or block the Agency’s number in WhatsApp.

5. Sharing and service providers

We share personal data only with:

  • Google Cloud / Firebase (Google LLC) — hosting, database (Cloud Firestore), file storage, authentication and server functions.
  • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform) — sending WhatsApp messages and managing templates, as described above, when an Agency connects WhatsApp.
  • The Agency itself — its authorised users see the data according to the roles and permissions the Agency sets.
  • Authorities — when required by law, court order or to protect rights, safety and security.
  • A successor — if PolicyOps is merged or transferred, under the same protections.

When a customer chooses to contact an Agency through email or the WhatsApp app on their own device, that exchange is handled by those apps, not by PolicyOps.

6. Where data is stored

Data is stored on Google Cloud infrastructure. The database is located in the United States (us-central1) and server functions run in India (asia-south1) and the United States. Data may therefore be transferred outside India, which we do only as permitted by applicable law and with safeguards provided by our service providers.

7. Security

  • All traffic uses HTTPS (TLS encryption in transit); data is encrypted at rest by Google Cloud.
  • Each Agency’s data is kept separate by organization, and access inside an Agency is limited by roles and permissions.
  • WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted with a key held in a secret manager and used only on the server.
  • New organizations must be approved before they can use the service.

No system is completely secure. If we become aware of a breach affecting personal data, we will notify the affected Agency and authorities as required by law.

8. Retention

  • Agency data is kept while the Agency’s account is active, and deleted or returned when the Agency asks or closes its account, except where we must keep it longer by law.
  • Financial and ledger records may be kept for the period required by Indian accounting and tax laws.
  • Message logs are kept for as long as the Agency keeps its account, so it can see its message history, unless it asks for them to be deleted sooner.
  • Backups and logs are removed on their normal cycle after deletion.

9. Your rights

Subject to applicable law (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023), you may ask to access, correct, update or erase your personal data, withdraw consent, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. You may also raise a grievance with us and, if not resolved, with the Data Protection Board of India.

  • Policyholders: please contact the insurance agency that holds your policy; we will help the Agency respond. You can also write to us and we will pass your request to the Agency.
  • Agency users: you can update your profile in the app or ask your Agency administrator or us.

10. Data deletion

To request deletion of your data, email rbit.apps@gmail.com with the subject “Data deletion request”, your name, the mobile number or email address used, and the agency name if you know it. We confirm the request, verify it, and delete the data (or arrange for the Agency to do so) within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep some of it, in which case we tell you what was kept and why.

  • An Agency can delete customers, policies, documents and templates inside PolicyOps, and can disconnect its WhatsApp Business Account in Settings → WhatsApp (or from the WhatsApp Business app), after which we stop using its access token and delete it.
  • To close an Agency account and delete all of its data, the Agency administrator can write to the same address.

11. Cookies and local storage

PolicyOps uses only what is needed to run the app: browser storage set by Firebase Authentication to keep you signed in, and small local settings (for example screen preferences). We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

12. Children

PolicyOps is a business tool for Agencies and is not meant for children. An Agency may record details of minors only as part of an insurance policy (for example as an insured member or nominee), under its own responsibility and with the consent required by law.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date above shows the latest version; for significant changes we will notify Agencies in the app or by email.
